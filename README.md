# 🧠 Mentallico

Mentallico is a **mental health support and awareness platform** designed as a graduation project. The platform aims to provide **safe, accessible, and educational mental health resources**, combining technology, data analysis, and user-centered design to help users better understand their mental well-being.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer**: Mentallico is not a replacement for professional medical advice or diagnosis. It is an educational and supportive platform.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Mentallico focuses on early awareness, guidance, and community support for mental health. The system integrates:

* Educational content (articles & blog posts)
* Data-driven mental health insights
* AI-assisted analysis (future expansion)
* Trusted professional recommendations

The project is built with a strong emphasis on **ethical AI**, **user privacy**, and **scientific mental health foundations**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Raise awareness about common mental health conditions
* Help users recognize early signs and symptoms
* Provide reliable resources and coping strategies
* Support students and young adults in understanding mental health
* Apply data science and software engineering concepts in a real-world problem

---

## 👥 Target Audience

* Students and young adults
* Individuals seeking mental health awareness
* People looking for verified educational resources
* Researchers and educators interested in mental health data

---

## 🧩 Key Features

### 📝 Educational Blog

* Articles about mental health conditions (e.g., anxiety, depression, stress)
* Coping techniques and self-care practices
* Myth-busting mental health misconceptions
* Real-life inspired scenarios (non-identifiable)

### 📊 Data Analysis & Classification

* Structured mental health datasets
* Categorization of mental health conditions by:

  * Symptoms
  * Severity levels
  * Risk factors
* Support for future ML models (classification & prediction)

### 🤖 AI & ML (Planned / Optional)

* Sentiment analysis on user text (optional)
* Risk-level classification (low / medium / high)
* Early warning insights (non-diagnostic)

### 🧑‍⚕️ Doctors & Professionals Directory

* Verified mental health professionals
* User recommendations & reviews (moderated)
* Specializations and contact methods

### 🌐 Community Section

* Awareness posts and shared experiences
* Educational discussions (moderated)
* Mental health campaigns
